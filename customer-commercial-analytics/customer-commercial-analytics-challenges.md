# Customer & Commercial Analytics - SQL Challenges

Dataset: E-Commerce Customer Behavior Dataset V2
Tool: MySQL Workbench
Table: ecommerce_customer_data

---

## Q1. Customer Count
Find the total number of unique customers.

## Q2. Total Orders
Find the total number of unique orders.

## Q3. Total Revenue
Calculate the total revenue using `Total_Amount`.

## Q4. Revenue by Product Category
Find total revenue and total quantity sold for each product category.

## Q5. Top 10 Customers by Revenue
Find the 10 customers who generated the highest total revenue.

## Q6. Average Order Value
Calculate the average order value (AOV).

Formula: Total Revenue / Number of Orders

## Q7. Returning vs Non-Returning Customers
Compare total revenue generated by returning customers and non-returning customers.

## Q8. Customer Purchase Frequency
Find the number of orders placed by each customer and identify customers with more than 3 orders.

## Q9. Top Customer Within Each City
Find the highest-revenue customer in every city.

## Q10. First and Latest Purchase
For every customer, find:
- First purchase date
- Latest purchase date
- Number of orders
- Total revenue

## Q11. Days Between Customer Purchases
For each customer, calculate the number of days between their current purchase and previous purchase.

## Q12. Discount Impact
Compare total revenue and average order value (AOV) for orders with and without discounts. Also calculate the total discount given.

## Q13. Discount by Category
For each product category, calculate:
- Total discount
- Total revenue
- Average discount per order

## Q14. Top 5 Categories by Revenue
Rank product categories by total revenue and return the top 5 categories.

## Q15. Delivery Time vs Customer Rating
Calculate the average customer rating for each delivery-time bucket:
- 1-3 days
- 4-7 days
- 8+ days

## Q16. Device Behaviour
Compare different device types based on:
- Number of orders
- Total revenue
- Average order value

## Q17. Customer Revenue Ranking
Rank every customer by total revenue using `DENSE_RANK()` and identify the top 10 customers.

## Q18. Customer Revenue Contribution
Calculate what percentage of total revenue each customer contributes.

Formula: Customer Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
